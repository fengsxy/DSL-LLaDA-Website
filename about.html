DSL-LLaDA: Scaling Continuous Denoising to 8B Masked Diffusion LMs

DSL-LLaDA lightly adapts LLaDA-8B-Instruct with Discrete Stochastic Localization, allowing response positions to evolve in continuous embedding space before the model commits to final tokens.

Delay the hard decision.

Standard LLaDA sampling repeatedly reveals discrete tokens. DSL-LLaDA keeps every response position continuous, revises the full state jointly, and performs a hard decode only after denoising.
