Abstract.

Few-step masked diffusion decoding tends to choose between short outputs and long, repetitive ones. DSL-LLaDA targets that failure mode without training a continuous language model from scratch.

We lightly adapt LLaDA-8B-Instruct with Discrete Stochastic Localization (DSL), replacing binary masking with continuous per-token Gaussian noise as a soft mask. The adapted model evolves all response positions jointly in embedding space and defers hard token commitment to the final step.

On zero-shot summarization at 16 or fewer forward passes, DSL-LLaDA-SDE achieves the best ROUGE-1 on all four benchmarks and largely avoids the premature-termination and repetition tradeoff of iterative unmasking. The same adaptation also produces selective noisy-state robustness: corrupted tokens can be corrected while clean tokens remain unchanged.

Delay the hard decision.

Standard LLaDA sees clean tokens or a hard mask. DSL-LLaDA replaces that binary input with graded per-token uncertainty and maps the resulting soft state back into the pretrained backbone.
